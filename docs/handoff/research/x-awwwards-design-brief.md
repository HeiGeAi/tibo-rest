# Design research brief (X + Awwwards)

User asked to study excellent sites on X then overhaul frontend.

## Patterns to steal (lounge direction)
- Near-black / warm brown base, ONE warm accent
- Oversized editorial typography (serif display + sans body)
- ONE slowly moving atmospheric focal (grain, light wash) — not fireworks
- Scroll-triggered reveals with shared motion language (one ease, duration scale)
- Smooth scroll (Lenis) synced to ScrollTrigger
- Small tactile controls: pills, progress dots, circular + buttons
- Film grain / halftone texture at low opacity
- prefers-reduced-motion respected
- Animate only transform/opacity (and careful clip-path)

## Named references
- Forms / forms.world — SplitText + ScrollTrigger
- MIRA / trymira.com — cinematic, warm CTA, outlined sticky nav
- Studio K95 — spatial/3D (only if lightweight; optional later)
- Aspen Search — noise, geometric panels, counters
- Zephyr (himanshubuildss) — dark olive, floating focal
- RedNova AI — slow warm looping background
- Mat Voyce / matvoyce.tv — kinetic type
- Ribbit, More Nutrition, Design by Dylan — GSAP agency craft
- HeiGe-UI examples: neon-nightshift.html, editorial-noir.html

## What failed in previous delivery
- Looked like patched template / AI lounge kit
- Motion added but still not "authored" narrative
